When standard camera or image decoding fails because a barcode is scratched, faded, smudged, low-contrast, or tilted, the recovery pipeline tests 40 horizontal scanlines with guard pattern verification and strict multi-scanline consensus to safely read the code.

Safety Rule: The pipeline only accepts a result when evidence is unambiguous. If a barcode is too damaged, it safely abstains rather than inventing an incorrect product code.

    Printing Guidelines for Reliable Scanning

    • Paper Size: Select A4 (210 × 297 mm).
    • Scale Setting: Always choose 100% / Actual Size. Do not use "Fit to Printable Area" or "Shrink to Fit", as scaling distorts narrow barcode bars.
    • Quality: Standard or high quality on white paper or sticker sheets.
    • Cutting: Cut along the light-gray dashed guidelines with scissors or a paper trimmer.
